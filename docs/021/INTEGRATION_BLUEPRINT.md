## Mandatory strategies: explicit proposed behavior, distinguish unsaid policy

**Time-based:** configure `(exchange,token,product=INTRADAY,side,qty)` from verified catalogue. At 09:15 Asia/Kolkata initiate one risk-approved entry per configured trading session **only if gate RUNNING, feed/broker evidence fresh, session valid**. Persist session+strategy trigger identity before dispatch; no duplicate on restart/repeated ticks. At 15:15 disable new entries, cancel this strategy's working orders (cancel is async), reconcile fills, and submit only risk-authorized reducing orders for **its remaining filled, attributed open exposure**. Consult broker `squareOffQuantity` for account-level truth and use it as a bound/cross-check, not as ownership. When other strategies offset account net, flag close semantics unresolved, never manufacture internal executions. Do not initiate a late catch-up entry by default; document as provisional policy needing sponsor clarification. End-of-day unresolved orders/exposure are explicitly UNKNOWN/FAILED, not ‘exited’.

**Open breakout:** each trading session capture **official daily open** from `ltpo` snapshot or `full` packet; verify same exchange/token/session, positive value and freshness. Trigger at most one configured directional entry on LTP >= open*1.01 (BUY) or <= open*0.99 (SELL); if both thresholds crossed at different times after entry, never create second risk-increasing position without deliberate reset rule. AFTER attributed fill, derive actual WAP from ledger, not intended limit or signal LTP. Long target WAP*1.05 and stop WAP*0.95; short target WAP*0.95 and stop WAP*1.05. Compute broker limit price in paise rounded to instrument ticksize under an explicit safety policy; app-managed exits are **normal broker orders** passing the central safety gate (authorized reducing route under STOPPED/KILLING as defined). Partial entry fills create partial risk and require exit sizing against actual filled residual, cancel outstanding entry first where needed; prevent simultaneous duplicate TP/SL exits; one atomic exit intent/trigger per residual generation; late fills restart reconciliation. This is a proposal for precise behavior where guide does not prescribe re-entry, order type, simultaneous pending fills or overnight policy: document choices and fail closed on ambiguity.

**Third custom strategy:** use existing EMA crossover (3/5 closes on 1m) or other proven built-in, with **written** symbol selection, warmup, entry, quantity, max exposure, exit on opposite cross **or** 15:15 close; inspect actual implementation to ensure claimed rules match tested behavior. Do not claim a custom exit exists until implemented. Judges may check exact written reference rule against output. Prefer fixed NSE cash 1-share controlled demo initially.
